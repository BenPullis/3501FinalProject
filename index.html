Calculate Your Climate Impact

A Simple Carbon Footprint Estimator

Climate change is often discussed at a global scale, but personal choices also play a role. This project helps estimate how transportation, diet, home energy, and consumption can add up to your annual carbon footprint.

Use this calculator as an educational tool to better understand your current impact and where practical changes could lower emissions over time.

The results are estimates based on simple emission factors and are meant for classroom learning, not a formal carbon audit.
